# Training Center Management Application

## Users

- **Administrator** — Manages the entire platform, including users, training programs, schedules, payments, attendance, and reports.
- **Trainer** — Manages assigned courses, schedules, attendance, grades, and student evaluations.
- **Student** — Views available courses, enrolls in training, checks their schedule, attendance, grades, payments, and certificates.

## Features

- **Training/Course Management** — Create, update, and manage training programs, including course names, descriptions, duration, capacity, trainers, and schedules.

- **Student Management** — Add and manage student profiles, personal information, enrollment history, attendance records, grades, and certificates.

- **Enrollment/Registration Management** — Allow students to register for available courses and enable administrators to approve, manage, and track enrollments.

- **Course Scheduling** — Create and manage class schedules, including dates, times, classrooms, trainers, and assigned courses. Students and trainers can view their schedules.

- **Attendance Management** — Record and monitor student attendance for each course session. Trainers can mark students as present, absent, or late, while administrators can access attendance reports.

- **Payment Management** — Track course fees, student payments, payment status, outstanding balances, and payment history.

- **Grades and Assessments** — Allow trainers to record exam results, assignments, and evaluations. Students can view their grades and overall performance.

- **PDF Certificate Generation** — Automatically generate professional PDF certificates for students who successfully complete a training program.

## Bonus Feature

- **QR Code Attendance** — Generate a unique QR code for each course session. Students scan the QR code using their phone to quickly register their attendance, reducing manual work for trainers.
